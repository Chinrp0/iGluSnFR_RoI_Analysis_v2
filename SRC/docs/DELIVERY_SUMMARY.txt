SCIENTIFIC VALUE SUMMARY
========================================================================

Your current data shows:
- Similar recruitment (WT: 17.4%, R213W: 17.3% active)
- R213W fires faster (0.029 Hz vs 0.021 Hz, p=0.014)
- Similar amplitudes (both ~0.05 dF/F)

New modules will reveal:

1. IEI Analysis
   Q: Does R213W fire more regularly or randomly?
   A: CV < 1 = regular, CV = 1 = random, CV > 1 = bursting

2. Amp-Freq Correlation
   Q: Are faster-firing ROIs larger or smaller?
   A: Positive r = coupled, negative r = inverse, r≈0 = independent

3. Event Raster
   Q: Is R213W activity synchronized across population?
   A: Clustered events = synchronized, uniform = asynchronous

4. Biological Variability
   Q: Is the phenotype consistent across replicates?
   A: Low CV = reproducible, high CV = variable

5. Recruitment Curves
   Q: Does R213W have more high-frequency ROIs?
   A: Divergence at high thresholds = more active ROIs

6. Cumulative Events
   Q: Does activity change during recording?
   A: Linear = stable, curved = rundown/buildup
